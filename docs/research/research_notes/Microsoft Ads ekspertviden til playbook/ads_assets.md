## 1. Responsive search ads (RSA): limits, pinning, ad strength, ads per ad group, ETA retirement, dynamic text, AI-generated assets

### Takeaway
The hard limits are unchanged and confirmed by the API docs: 3–15 headlines of up to 30 characters, 2–4 descriptions of up to 90 characters, two 15-character paths, and the domain plus paths no longer than 67 characters. Pinning must leave at least one asset that can fill each position. An ad group can have 3 active RSAs.

The big 2026 changes concern AI-generated assets:
- **January 15, 2026:** autogenerated RSA assets are switched on by default for new RSAs.
- **April 2026:** they are being extended to existing RSAs that don't use all 15 headlines and 4 descriptions.
- **New ad strength model:** ad strength now has a 0–100 % model with concrete targets, but Microsoft itself says it "doesn't guarantee performance results".

### Cited Findings

**Hard limits (API)**
- RSA: "between 3-15 headlines" and "between 2-4 descriptions"; each must contain at least one word. Max 30 "final characters … after substitution" per headline and 90 per description (input up to 1,000 characters including dynamic text). Double-width characters (CJK and **emojis**) halve the limits to 15/45. No newline characters. IF functions are allowed. — [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd, Learn API, acc. 2026-11-20](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- Path1/Path2: max 15 "final (not dynamic text)" characters each. The ad fails to display (or uses default text) if the final-URL domain plus both paths exceed 67 characters. Paths cannot contain "/" or newline, a space is shown as "_", Path2 only if Path1 is set, and paths may contain IF functions and `{MatchType}`-style parameters. — [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- The display-URL domain "will be generated from the domain of your Final URL". A separate `Domain` (display URL) element exists but is "Reserved for limited pilot usage e.g. pharmaceutical customers". Help: a Display URL option is only for pharmaceutical advertisers, US only. — [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13); [OFF] [About Responsive Search ads, help mirror upd. 2026-07-27](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)
- RSAs can only be created in ad groups with AdGroupType "SearchStandard", not "SearchDynamic". — [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- Headlines and descriptions are stored as account-level text assets that are shared across ads, with the same asset ID for the same text. — [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- **Conflict, UI vs API:** the help page says that when you enter assets manually "you can have between five and fifteen headlines and up to four descriptions". With autogenerated assets on, "you must still provide at least three headlines and two descriptions". The API says 3–15. — [OFF] [About Responsive Search ads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads) vs [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- What is shown: up to 3 headlines and 2 descriptions, and "descriptions might not always be shown". — [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- General policy: "A combined total of at least 3 words is required in your title and descriptive text". Character limits are calculated with dynamic text included. — [OFF] [General Ad Requirements, upd. Aug 31, 2026](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)

**Pinning**
- Pin values: Headline1/2/3 and Description1/2. "At least one eligible TextAsset must be available for each headline position, so you cannot pin all of the assets to the same position". For example, 3 pinned to H1 and 3 to H2 is fine as long as at least one is unpinned or pinned to H3. "Unless you have a specific requirement for an asset, don't pin it". To unpin, update the ad and leave PinnedField empty. — [OFF] [AssetLink, Learn API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/assetlink?view=bingads-13)
- Best practice: "Avoid pinning headlines or descriptions, if possible. Pinning restricts the number of headline or description combinations". — [OFF] [Best practices for RSAs, help mirror upd. 2026-07-27](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_rsa_bestpractices)
- **Internal contradiction:** the FAQ on the RSA help page says "We recommend that you pin positions 1 and 2 for headlines and position 1 for descriptions", then adds that pinning restricts combinations. — [OFF] [About Responsive Search ads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)

**Ad strength and asset labels (two different things)**
- *Ad strength:* rated "Poor", "Average", "Good" or "Excellent" and rates the "diversity, quality, and quantity" of headlines and descriptions. Microsoft claims: "Advertisers who improve their ad strength score from 'Poor' to 'Excellent' see 33% more CTR … on average". Ad strength includes autogenerated assets if they are enabled. — [OFF] [About Responsive Search ads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)
- *New ad strength model (rolling out; "Not everyone has this feature yet"):*
  - Scored 0–100 %: Poor 0–50, Average 51–60, Good 61–70, Excellent 71–100. Text and image scores are weighted equally.
  - "Good score" targets for RSA text: 9–12 headlines; 3 descriptions; average headline length 17–24 characters; average description length 46–85 characters; a "popular keyword" in 25–75 % of headlines and of descriptions; 66–99 % of headlines and descriptions "relevant" to the landing page; "Pinned headlines 1—7"; "Pinned descriptions 1".
  - In grids and the API the score can take up to 24 hours to update.
  - "The score helps guide improvements but doesn't guarantee performance results."
  - — [OFF] [About Ad strength, help mirror upd. 2026-07-27](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_adstrength)
- *Asset performance label* (per headline or description): Low / Good / Best / Unrated / Learning. — [OFF] [AssetLink](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/assetlink?view=bingads-13)
- Does ad strength predict results? On Google data, no. Optmyzr's study of 22K+ accounts and 1M+ ads found "no clear correlation between ad performance and Ad Strength". RSAs rated 'average' had the best CPA, conversion rate and ROAS; most ads are 'poor' or 'average' "but perform well". — [3P] [Optmyzr Ad Strength & Creative Study, Sep 9, 2024](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/google-ad-strength-study/). No equivalent independent Microsoft-account study was found.

**Ads per ad group**
- Limit: "Each ad group can contain 3 active responsive search ads", 1–100 ads of all types (active plus paused), and 3 active (5 total) multimedia ads. Microsoft's general advice is "a small number of ads, for example 3 ads per ad group". — [OFF] [Entity Limits, Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/entity-hierarchy-limits?view=bingads-13)
- "We recommend you have a minimum of one active responsive search ad in an ad group … limit of three enabled responsive search ads per ad group." — [OFF] [Best practices for RSAs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_rsa_bestpractices)
- Study: "ad groups with two RSAs experience a surge in conversion rate that single-RSA and three-RSA ad groups don't … 2 RSA ads per ad group seems to be the sweet spot". This is a 2022 study of 13,671 Google Ads accounts. — [3P] [Optmyzr RSA study, published Apr 7, 2023](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/optmyzr-study-responsive-search-ad-performance/)

**Expanded and standard text ads (retired)**
- Advertisers "can no longer create new or edit existing" expanded text ads (ETA) or standard text ads. Existing ETAs keep serving and can be paused, enabled or removed. "Standard Text ads stopped serving in February 2025." — [OFF] [FAQ – Expanded text ads, help mirror upd. 2026-07-27](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_exta_faq)
- The cut-off for creating and editing ETAs was February 1, 2023, after being postponed from August 29, 2022. — [SNIPPET] [Microsoft blog Aug 2022 "deadline extended to February 1 2023" (search result)](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/august-2022/you-have-5-additional-months-to-migrate-to-responsive-search-ads-deadline-extended-to-february-1-2023); [SNIPPET] [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/microsoft-advertising-rsas-deadline-extended-again-34005.html)

**Dynamic text**
- Parameters `{keyword}`, `{param1}`, `{param2}` and `{param3}`. Each `{keyword}` must include default text (`{keyword:Default}`), and the "default text cannot contain a dynamic text parameter". If substitution would exceed the limit, the defaults are used for all parameters at once. Ads with dynamic text that is too long "won't display". — [OFF] [Dynamic text parameters, help mirror upd. 2026-07-27](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutparameters)
- Capitalisation of the placeholder controls the output: `{KeyWord}`/`{KEYWORD}` capitalises each word, `{keyword}` gives lower case, `{Keyword}` capitalises the first word only, plus `{KeyWORD}` and `{KEYWord}`. These options are supported for **Danish**. Policy reminder on the same page: you "can't have more than one fully capitalized word in your ad title or ad text". — [OFF] [Capitalization of the keyword placeholder, help mirror](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutparameterskeyword)
- Policy: dynamic text must not create incorrect grammar or spelling, and "If you bid on misspelled keywords, do not use them in ad groups that contain {keyword} dynamic text." — [OFF] [General Ad Requirements, upd. Aug 31, 2026](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)
- Countdown for RSAs and multimedia ads: `{COUNTDOWN(2021-12-12 11:59:59,5)}` uses the viewer's time zone and `{GLOBAL_COUNTDOWN(…)}` the account's time zone. The last number is how many days before the end the ad starts (default 5, max 999). The language follows the language the ad is served in. The ad stops after the end but keeps status Enabled. — [OFF] [Show a countdown, help mirror](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_countdown)
- Location insertion: `{LOCATION(City|State|Country):Default text}` in headlines, descriptions and paths of RSAs (and of multimedia ads, still rolling out). — [OFF] [Location insertion, help mirror](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivelocation)
- Best practice: "Use a single dynamic keyword insertion"; include at least 1 brand headline; "at least 3 other headlines that don't include your keywords". — [OFF] [Best practices for RSAs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_rsa_bestpractices)
- Study: DKI and ad customizers "increases the impressions per ad" but there is "a decrease in conversions per ad" (432,343 Google ads). — [3P] [Optmyzr RSA study, 2023](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/optmyzr-study-responsive-search-ad-performance/)

**AI-generated assets and Copilot (changes in 2026)**
- **Jan 15, 2026:** "Autogenerated assets" is switched on automatically for **new** RSAs globally (except China and South Korea). It generates extra text assets only "if you have not provided all the available text fields (15 headlines, 4 descriptions)" — with the maximum supplied, nothing is generated. Existing RSAs are not changed retroactively. Imports keep the source setting. It applies to text only. Sensitive verticals stay opt-in (Autos, Financial Services, Health, Legal, Travel and others; furniture is not listed). Microsoft claims +5 % CTR. Opt out by unchecking the box when creating the RSA, or via Campaigns > Edit > Edit auto-generated assets settings. Delete generated assets in the Assets tab using the Source column. — [OFF] [Microsoft Advertising blog, Jan 15, 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/january-2026/performance-max-updates-and-other-product-news-for-january-2026)
- **Apr 16, 2026:** "we are bringing Autogenerated assets to **existing** Responsive Search ads without the maximum text assets supplied by users". Same exclusions. Opt out in campaign settings or through the autogenerated-assets notification. — [OFF] [Microsoft Advertising blog, Apr 16, 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/april-2026/performance-max-updates-and-other-product-news-for-april-2026)
- Help: "Autogenerated assets are enabled by default". They are campaign-level (Settings > "Automatically generate assets for my Responsive Search ads") and built "from your landing page (Final URL)". Deleting an autogenerated asset removes it from all campaigns, ad groups and ads in the account. — [OFF] [About Responsive Search ads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)
- Copilot in the platform "helps you … generate assets and create ads". — [OFF] [Copilot in Microsoft Advertising Platform, help mirror upd. 2026-09-02](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_copilotmsaplatform)
- Messaging guidelines: up to 40 rules (plus up to 25 term exclusions) that steer AI-written copy in **Performance Max and AI Max for Search** (pilot, around Oct 2026). — [SNIPPET] [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/microsoft-advertising-sets-rules-for-ai-generated-ads-488429); [SNIPPET] [ppcnewsfeed, Oct 2026](https://ppcnewsfeed.com/ppc-news/2026-10/microsoft-adds-messaging-guidelines-to-text-guardrails/)
- AI Max for Search became generally available in Aug 2026 as an "optional suite". — [OFF] [Microsoft Advertising blog, Aug 31, 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/august-2026/ai-max-for-search-and-other-product-news-for-august-2026)
- RSA recommendations ("Apply" a recommended RSA) go through review that "typically takes less than three hours (but could take up to one business day)". Edited text assets start serving "about five minutes after selecting Save". — [OFF] [About Responsive Search ads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)

### Inferences
- If the app writes fewer than 15 headlines or 4 descriptions in a campaign with autogenerated assets on (the default for new RSAs since Jan 2026, and for existing RSAs since the April 2026 rollout), Microsoft will add its own text taken from the landing page. That text bypasses the app's claim and evidence check and the Danish price-claim rules. The playbook should check, or propose turning off, the campaign's autogenerated-assets setting. Alternatively it can fill all 15 and 4 fields.
- The new ad strength targets (9–12 headlines, 3 descriptions, keyword in 25–75 % of assets) are Microsoft's own guide. They are not evidence of performance.

### Gaps
- No independent study of ad strength or pinning on *Microsoft* accounts was found; all large studies use Google Ads data.
- The exact rollout date of autogenerated assets for existing RSAs in Denmark is not stated beyond "April 2026".

## 2. Assets / ad extensions: limits, levels, precedence, scheduling, automated extensions and opt-out

### Takeaway
Extensions live in an account-level library and must be associated with the account, a campaign or an ad group. Exceptions: call extensions attach at campaign level only, and location extensions at account or campaign level only. Microsoft now says higher-level extensions can serve **alongside** lower-level ones of the same type, but an older note on the same page still says ad-group level overrides. Per-entity limits are mostly 20, with image 25 and logo/call 1. Automated extensions are on by default and can only be opted out at **account** level.

### Cited Findings

**Levels and precedence**
- The library is at account level, and an extension must be associated before it is eligible. "Call ad extensions can only be associated at the campaign level. Location ad extensions can only be associated at the account and campaign level." "When ad extensions from higher levels in your account are predicted to improve your performance, they are eligible to serve alongside extensions of the same type from lower levels." — [OFF] [Ad Extensions guide, Learn API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/ad-extensions?view=bingads-13); same sentence in [OFF] [About ad extensions, help mirror upd. 2026-09-30](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutadextensions)
- **Contradiction on the same help page:** "Any ad extensions associated at the ad group level will override ad extensions associated at the account or campaign levels." The sitelink help says "Sitelinks are eligible to serve for an ad at any level (ad group, campaign, and account)". — [OFF] [About ad extensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutadextensions); [OFF] [Sitelink extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addsitelinkextension)
- Extensions aren't supported for Audience campaigns. Serving depends on ad quality, extension quality, space, user signals, other extensions, and competitors' extensions. — [OFF] [About ad extensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutadextensions)
- Association limit per entity (API): Action 20, Call 1, Callout 20, Filter link 20, Flyer 20, **Image 25**, Location (all in account), Logo 1, Price 20, Promotion 20, Review 20, Sitelink 20, Structured snippet 20. An account can hold 200,000 sitelinks plus 150,000 other extensions. — [OFF] [Entity Limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/entity-hierarchy-limits?view=bingads-13). **Conflict:** the help table lists Image as **6**. — [OFF] [About ad extensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutadextensions). The image-extension help page says 25. — [OFF] [Image extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addimageextension)
- The API does not support partial update: optional fields left out of UpdateAdExtensions are deleted. A batch fails entirely if one extension fails. Max 100 extensions per call. — [OFF] [Ad Extensions guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/ad-extensions?view=bingads-13)

**Scheduling**
- Extensions cannot be paused; use scheduling or remove the association. "Ad extension scheduling is available for all extension types, except for image extensions." — [OFF] [Ad extension scheduling, help mirror upd. 2026-09-30](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutadextensionscheduling)

**Sitelinks**
- DisplayText max 25 characters if Description1/2 are set, otherwise 35. Description1 and Description2 are 35 characters each, and if you set one you must set the other. FinalUrls are required, the destination URL is deprecated, and custom parameters are limited to 3 (8 in a pilot). — [OFF] [SitelinkAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/sitelinkadextension?view=bingads-13)
- Up to 20 per campaign or ad group. "An ad can display two to eight sitelinks or dynamic sitelinks with descriptions, or two to six sitelinks in a single row without descriptions". Enhanced (description) sitelinks show only on "high-quality ads at the top". "Both lines of text must be used". — [OFF] [Sitelink extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addsitelinkextension). The API guide says "up to 10 links", and links earlier in the list get higher priority. — [OFF] [Ad Extensions guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/ad-extensions?view=bingads-13)
- Policy: the link text must make clear which part of the site it leads to; each sitelink needs unique text within the ad; no automatic downloads; a link to another site must name that site ("Buy on Contoso"). — [OFF] [Ad Extensions Policies, upd. Apr 8, 2026](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60206/-1)

**Callouts**
- 1–25 characters each. "The callout extension text must be different than the text of your ad." — [OFF] [CalloutAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/calloutadextension?view=bingads-13)
- 2–20 per entity. With one or fewer associated, "no callout text will serve". 2–4 are shown per impression. — [OFF] [Entity Limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/entity-hierarchy-limits?view=bingads-13)
- Policy: only specific selling points or deals, not "merely another line of text"; no emoticons or excessive punctuation; each must be unique. — [OFF] [Ad Extensions Policies](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60206/-1)

**Structured snippets**
- The header must be written in the language it will be shown in; there is a list of supported headers per language. Values are max 25 characters. — [OFF] [StructuredSnippetAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/structuredsnippetadextension?view=bingads-13)
- One snippet per impression with 3–10 values; it serves on desktop and tablet. — [OFF] [Ad Extensions guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/ad-extensions?view=bingads-13). **Conflict:** the help text says "two to 10 values", while its own table says a minimum of 3. — [OFF] [Structured snippet help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addstructuredsnippetextension)
- Policy: no promotional text in values; values must relate to the header and not repeat it; the products must actually be offered. — [OFF] [Ad Extensions Policies](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60206/-1)

**Promotion**
- Fields: PromotionItem (max 20 characters), PercentOff or MoneyAmountOff (+ CurrencyCode, **DKK supported**), OrdersOverAmount (minimum order), PromotionCode (max 15), DiscountModifier (`None` / `UpTo`, giving "Up to …"), PromotionStartDate/EndDate (the dates displayed), PromotionOccasion, and Language (**Danish** supported). "If the end date has already passed for the current year, then each start and end date must be set for dates during the following year." — [OFF] [PromotionAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/promotionadextension?view=bingads-13); [OFF] [PromotionDiscountModifier](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/promotiondiscountmodifier?view=bingads-13)
- Occasion windows: BlackFriday and CyberMonday Oct 15–Dec 15; Christmas Nov 1–Jan 15; NewYears Dec 1–Feb 28; None, WinterSale, SummerSale, EndOfSeason and YearEndGift can run any time. — [OFF] [PromotionOccasion](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/promotionoccasion?view=bingads-13)
- Serving logic: with an occasion and no schedule it serves every year in the occasion window; with a schedule it serves during the scheduled dates. "You can add one minimum purchase amount and/or one promotion code." The landing page must include details of the promotion. — [OFF] [Promotion extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addpromotionextension)
- Policy: the promo code must work; no restriction to an audience ("first-time customers", birthdays); applied at checkout; no promotions for normally free items; no extra costs to offset the discount; "Promotion titles must be clear, concise, accurate". — [OFF] [Ad Extensions Policies](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60206/-1)
- **Conflict on availability:** the API guide says "Promotion Extensions are available for customers in AU, CA, DE, FR, US and UK" — [OFF] [Ad Extensions guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/ad-extensions?view=bingads-13). The help page says "Ability to set up in: All Bing markets. Serves in: All Bing Markets" — [OFF] [Promotion extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addpromotionextension).

**Price**
- 3–8 table rows. PriceExtensionType is one of Brands, Events, Locations, Neighborhoods, ProductCategories, ProductTiers, ServiceCategories, Services, ServiceTiers. — [OFF] [PriceAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/priceadextension?view=bingads-13)
- Header 25 characters, description 25 characters. Shown "only on ads listed at the very top". Prices are reviewed against the landing page. Not available in China, Taiwan or Hong Kong. — [OFF] [Price extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addpriceextension)
- Policy: prices and products must be visible on the landing page; header and description must be related, and the description must not repeat the header. — [OFF] [Ad Extensions Policies](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60206/-1)

**Image**
- Alt text max 35 characters. DisplayText max 35 (required for the SearchMultiple layout). Layouts are SearchSingle and SearchMultiple. — [OFF] [ImageAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/imageadextension?view=bingads-13)
- Minimum image 703×368 px at 1.91:1, auto-cropped to other ratios. Multi-image needs at least 5 images and at least 4 image extensions, and doesn't serve with sitelink, price or certain other cards. Up to 1,000 campaigns and 1,000 ad groups per account can have image associations. **Autogenerated image extensions are applied to ad groups without image extensions** (still rolling out). — [OFF] [Image extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addimageextension)

**Logo**
- One per level. 1:1 ratio, at least 128×128 px, max 5,120 KB. Business name max 25 characters. Domain max 50 characters (a domain, not a URL). — [OFF] [LogoAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/logoadextension?view=bingads-13)
- "Starting on March 17, 2025, we'll automatically add one account-level logo extension to new and existing accounts", unless you opted out of automated business logos or already have logo extensions. — [OFF] [Logo extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addlogoextension)

**Action**
- A predefined ActionType plus Language (Danish supported) sets the button text; not every type exists in every language. Account, campaign or ad group level. — [OFF] [ActionAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/actionadextension?view=bingads-13); [OFF] [Action extensions help](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addactionextension)

**Call**
- Country code (DK supported); number max 35 characters and valid for the country. Overrides the location-extension number. Call tracking is US/UK only (API). — [OFF] [CallAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/calladextension?view=bingads-13)

## 4. Editorial policies relevant to a Danish B2B/B2C furniture & equipment shop; statuses, review time, appeals

### Takeaway
Policy hot-spots for this shop:
- **Capitalisation** must be grammatical and consistent: no random or ALL-CAPS words, at most one fully capitalised word, and descriptions in sentence case.
- **Punctuation:** max one "!" or "?" per sentence and no consecutive symbols.
- **Prices:** "Only one price or % allowed per ad". EU prices must include VAT and fees and state unit price and freight.
- **Urgency:** no urgency phrases.
- **Claims and comparisons:** any claim or comparison must be substantiated in the ad or on the landing page (with source and date for comparisons).
- **Trademarks:** fair use is allowed (resellers, comparisons).

Most reviews finish within 48 hours. Updated ads are effectively paused until re-approved. Appeals are made per ad or asset in the UI, or via the API (2,000 per day).

### Cited Findings

**Style (General Ad Requirements, last updated Aug 31, 2026)** — [OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)
- "Ads must use proper, grammatically correct capitalization … Capitalization must be consistent … may not use excessive or random capitalization, such as BIG SALE or AmAzinG."
- "Titles should be title-cased in English-speaking markets. Titles should be sentence-cased in German-, French-, Spanish-, Italian-, and Dutch-speaking markets. Descriptions must be sentence case." Danish is not named. Legitimate acronyms and all-caps registered brands are allowed.
- "One exclamation point or question mark is allowed per sentence in ad text." "Consecutive, non-alphanumeric characters are not allowed" (e.g. "!!!").
- "**Only one price or % allowed per ad.** This includes discounts and price reductions, such as 25% 50% off! or $99.99 $89.99 $79.99!"
- Also banned: italic, bold or superscript fonts; ™/® unless the trademark is registered or applied for; dingbats, arrows, bullets, emoticons, math symbols such as <, > and =, and @ or § used as decoration. Allowed: &, %, +, #, x.
- "Not utilize language that implies urgency such as 'do this immediately,' 'supplies are running out,' 'offer won't last.'" "Be clear and factual." No misleading CTAs.
- Clickbait is banned ("use this 1 weird tip"). Ads may not imply knowledge of the user ("Attention Bing Users").
- Language: "The language used in the ad must be appropriate for the market"; ad and landing-page language must match; landing pages should be in the local language.
- The help centre adds: "For ads to pass the review process, they must be written in the same language as the ad group language and market setting." — [OFF] [The editorial review, help mirror](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_editprocess)

**Prices and claims**
- Pricing policy (last updated Sep 30, 2026):
  - "Ads must state when conditions apply". Offers must match the landing page. Prices that depend on options or taxes must give the baseline ("Flights to London **from** $499" allowed; "**for** $499" not).
  - EU market block: "Unit price should be included in all applicable offers. All relevant taxes and fees should be included in price displayed … Information on freight, delivery … shipping cost … must be provided".
  - The "lowest price in 30 days" rule for lowered prices appears under another market block (Montenegro) in Microsoft's page, not under the EU block.
  - — [OFF] [Pricing policy](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60381/-1)
- Misleading content (last updated Apr 8, 2026): "Advertising must not have unproven claims or endorsements. If an ad makes a claim … it must provide substantiation of the claim either in the ad or at the ad destination." It also bans fake urgency and "Fraudulent free offers or pricing claims". — [OFF] [Information Integrity and Misleading Content](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60357/-1)
- Trademarks (last updated Jun 10, 2026):
  - Fair use is allowed: resellers of authentic goods (the landing page must show how to buy), review and comparison sites, and dictionary use.
  - "Comparative advertising is permitted. Claims must be substantiated", e.g. the destination must "cite the source and date of the claim".
  - Owners complain via a form; Microsoft does not pre-vet. Some abuse-prone terms may be restricted.
  - Counterfeit modifiers ("replica", "inspired by") are banned.
  - — [OFF] [Intellectual Property Policies](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60211/-1)
- Duplicate ads: Microsoft may disallow multiple ads from the same advertiser with too-similar sites (they need a distinct brand, look and feel, or products). — [OFF] [Duplicate Ads in Search, upd. Apr 8, 2026](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60279/-1)
- Policy change log for Sep–Nov 2026: Finance (EU CCD II), Gambling, US auto-dealer pricing. Nothing specific to retail ad copy. — [OFF] [Policies Change Log](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60225/-1)

**Review process and time**
- Every component (ad text, assets, keywords, extensions, customizers) is reviewed. Flagged items get a further review "which can take several hours" (triggers include double meanings, trademarks, and keywords not in the editorial database). "Most reviews are completed within 48 hours"; after two business days, contact support. Reviews are ongoing, so live ads can later be disapproved. Changing only http↔https does not trigger re-review. — [OFF] [The editorial review](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_editprocess)

**Statuses**

**Appeals**
- UI: "Request an exception" per ad or keyword, for all in the ad group, per extension, per asset, or for "All assets in this ad". Most are reviewed within 48 hours and the result is emailed. - Asset-level disapprovals (one bad headline no longer necessarily blocks the whole ad) are reported by third parties. — [SNIPPET] [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/microsoft-ads-introduces-asset-level-disapprovals-465521). A headline about appealing 5,000 disapproved ads at once — [SNIPPET, title only] [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/microsoft-advertising-lets-advertisers-appeal-5-000-disapproved-ads-at-once/)

**Common rejection causes and fixes (from the policies above)**
- ALL-CAPS words → sentence case.
- "!!" or "!" in several sentences → at most one per sentence.
- Several prices or percentages in one ad → keep one.
- "Kun i dag" / "Begrænset lager" ("only today" / "limited stock") urgency → remove, or use a promotion or countdown with real dates.
- Superlatives or comparisons ("Danmarks billigste", "Denmark's cheapest") without proof on the landing page → remove or add proof with source and date.
- Display URL domain differs from the landing page → align the domains.
- Final URL with æ/ø/å or other diacritics → use ASCII URLs.
- Language mismatch → Danish ad with a Danish ad group language and landing page.
- Sources: [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1); [Misleading content](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60357/-1); [IP](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60211/-1)

### Inferences
- Danish is not in Microsoft's title-case list. Danish orthography and the "Descriptions must be sentence case" rule make sentence case the safe default for Danish headlines too. Do not Title Case Danish.
- "Only one price or % allowed per ad" is ambiguous for RSAs (per asset or per served combination). A safe reading: don't put two different prices or percentages in one headline or description, and avoid price-ladder headlines.
- "Gratis" ("free"): allowed only if genuinely free and not a "fraudulent free offer". Free shipping must be shown on the landing page, with any threshold. EU pricing requires freight information.
- Danish law, not from Microsoft and unsourced here: B2C prices must include moms (VAT), and a "førpris" (previous price) must be the lowest price in the prior 30 days. B2B-only offers may be ex. moms if clearly stated. Microsoft's EU block itself says "All relevant taxes (including VAT)" should be included.

### Gaps
- The current policy text on **phone numbers in ad text** was not found in the Aug 2026 General Ad Requirements (only extension phone rules). The older "phone numbers" style section could not be verified.
- No explicit Microsoft rule on single superlatives ("bedst", "billigst" = "best", "cheapest") beyond the general substantiation requirement.
- Danish Consumer Ombudsman sources (forbrugerombudsmanden.dk) were not fetchable. The Danish legal points above are unsourced background.

## 5. Landing pages, final vs display URL, tracking templates, final URL suffix, mobile, redirects

### Takeaway
The final URL must work (no 4xx), resolve to a single static page, not block Microsoft's crawler, use ASCII-only characters, and share one base domain across the ad group. The display URL is derived from the final URL's domain, and redirects must land on the same top-level domain. Tracking templates must start with http(s), `{lpurl}` or `{unescapedlpurl}`. Lower levels override higher ones. Parallel tracking is mandatory.

### Cited Findings
- "The final URL must work properly and resolve to a single working website. It cannot connect to an email address or a file or result in a page with a 404 or other 4xx status code. The Microsoft Advertising Crawler should not be blocked … IP addresses are disallowed." "Display URLs and final URLs may only be in Latin, ASCII characters and must not contain accents or diacritics". "Display URLs in a single ad group must use the same top-level URL domain." "The display URL must match the landing page URL. If a tracking template is used to redirect the user, the landing page URL domain and content must match the final URL domain and content." Allowed mismatches are tracking redirects with a matching top-level domain, descriptive deep links, hosting sites (via appeal), and brand cases (via appeal). — [OFF] [General Ad Requirements, upd. Aug 31, 2026](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)
- Landing-page quality: no doorway or cloaking pages, no unexpected redirects to unrelated domains, no "product not available" pages, no sparse content. "Your final URL must resolve to a single static landing page". "All URLs within an ad group must use the same base domain." It must be your own or an authorised page, with no disruptive pop-ups. — [OFF] [Ad Relevance and Landing Page Quality, upd. Apr 8, 2026](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60215/-1)
- API URL rules: FinalUrls and FinalMobileUrls up to 2,048 characters (protocol included). Only the first URL in each list is used. Braces only for tags. They must start with http:// or https://. FinalMobileUrls require FinalUrls. A keyword-level final URL overrides the ad's. TrackingUrlTemplate is ≤ 2,048 characters, starts with http://, https://, `{lpurl}` or `{unescapedlpurl}`, and lower-level entities override higher. Undefined custom parameters are not validated (the placeholders are left in). UrlCustomParameters: max 8 on ads. — [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- Extensions: custom parameters max 3 (8 in a pilot). — [OFF] [SitelinkAdExtension](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/sitelinkadextension?view=bingads-13)
- Final URL suffix: parameters are "always sent to the landing page". It cannot begin with ?, & or #, cannot contain `{lpurl}`, and goes before any #. Microsoft recommends "placing tracking parameters that your landing page requires in a final URL suffix". — [OFF] [Using a final URL suffix, help mirror](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_upgradeurl_finalurlsuffix); [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)
- "Parallel tracking is mandatory for all accounts"; check that your click-measurement provider is compatible. — [OFF] [Parallel tracking, help mirror](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_paralleltracking)
- Changing only http↔https in a URL does not trigger editorial re-review. — [OFF] [The editorial review](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_editprocess)
- The ad strength model scores headline, description and image "relevance" to the landing page (target 66–99 %). Autogenerated assets and website images are taken from the final URL. — [OFF] [About Ad strength](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_adstrength); [OFF] [Multimedia ads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_mma_search)

### Inferences
- Danish URLs with æ/ø/å (e.g. `/møbler/` or a path "Kontormøbler") are likely to be rejected; paths are part of the display URL. Use ASCII (`moebler`, `kontormobler`) in final URLs and Path1/Path2.

## 6. Evidence on what makes ads perform (incl. Microsoft's own guidance on number of headlines and pinning)

### Takeaway
Microsoft's own advice:
- at least 8–10 distinct headlines, one brand headline, one DKI, and at least 3 non-keyword headlines
- distinct descriptions with benefits, a CTA, and shipping and return information
- avoid pinning
- judge an ad only after at least 1,000 impressions and at least one week

Independent data comes from Google accounts, not Microsoft:
- ad strength doesn't track results
- light pinning ≈ no pinning, while full pinning hurts conversion metrics
- 2 RSAs per ad group is the conversion-rate sweet spot
- concise headlines and sentence case did slightly better

### Cited Findings
- Microsoft best practice: "Aim to provide at least 8-10 distinct headlines that don't contain similar phrases. Use a combination of short and long headlines … use at least 1 brand title. Use a single dynamic keyword insertion. … at least 3 other headlines that don't include your keywords". Descriptions should give "benefits and features, a clear call to action, and shipping and return information." — [OFF] [Best practices for RSAs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_rsa_bestpractices)
- Microsoft testing guidance: check performance a week before and after; "let them run for at least one week or more … each ad should have a minimum of 1,000 impressions"; update the assets with the lowest impressions. — [OFF] [About Responsive Search ads](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)
- Microsoft's vendor claims: Poor→Excellent ad strength = +33 % CTR on average — [OFF] [About RSAs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads). Autogenerated assets = +5 % CTR — [OFF] [Blog Jan 15, 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/january-2026/performance-max-updates-and-other-product-news-for-january-2026)
- Pinning (Optmyzr 2024, Google, 22K accounts):
  - "Some pinning continues to be the winning strategy based on CPA (though no pinning is a close second), ROAS, and CPC. Conversion rates suffer when you pin."
  - Full pinning gives the best CTR only.
  - "pinning should not be done for complete control" — use it to secure creative in intended spots.
  - Headlines "benefit from concision", descriptions "from some length (but not too long)".
  - Sentence case "won" for RSAs, but the margin was close.
  - — [3P] [Optmyzr, Sep 9, 2024](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/google-ad-strength-study/)
- Pinning (Optmyzr 2022 data, 93,055 RSAs): pinning every position gives good CTR and conversion rate, but "impressions per ad group are 3.9 times higher" with multiple texts per pinned position. More headlines mean more impressions per RSA. Judge on incremental ad-group results, since RSAs "drive 4x the impressions". — [3P] [Optmyzr, Apr 7, 2023](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/optmyzr-study-responsive-search-ad-performance/)

### Inferences
- The app's testing rule (≥100 clicks, 2–4 weeks, judge on conversion rate and CPA) is compatible with, and stricter than, Microsoft's floor (≥1,000 impressions, ≥1 week).

### Gaps
- No Microsoft-account-specific A/B studies were found. All quantitative third-party evidence is from Google Ads, and PPC Land, WordStream, Adalysis, JumpFly and PPC Hero material was not fetched: ppc.land returned 403 and time was limited.

## Against the current rules

### Takeaway
Most of the app's ad rules in `src/ai/playbooks/microsoftAds.ts`, `src/ai/prompt.ts` and `src/ai/adCopy.ts` are confirmed. The important missing rules are:
- autogenerated RSA assets (on by default since 2026)
- "only one price or % per ad"
- ASCII-only URLs and paths (æ/ø/å)
- one base domain per ad group
- the API returning "Disapproved" for new ads still in review
- extension precedence (higher levels serve alongside lower)

A few rules are stated too strongly or are inaccurate: the ad-strength labels, any "!" counted as an error, and "the third RSA" treated as a secondary-source claim.

### Cited Findings

**Confirmed**

- The app's "keyword alone as headline 1, pinned" rule conflicts with Microsoft's "Avoid pinning … if possible" best practice. It fits Optmyzr's "some pinning" finding and Microsoft's own FAQ line "pin positions 1 and 2" ([OFF] [About RSAs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)). Keep it, but label it as the team's choice, not a Microsoft rule.
- "Don't chase ad strength; it didn't track results in large studies" (playbook, prompt) — **confirmed** ([3P] [Optmyzr 2024](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/google-ad-strength-study/)). Add that this is Google data. Microsoft itself says the score "doesn't guarantee performance results" ([OFF] [About Ad strength](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_adstrength)).
- "Two ads per ad group; that a third gives less we have only seen in secondary sources" — the first part is **confirmed**. **Correction:** the primary Optmyzr page itself says two-RSA ad groups get a conversion-rate "surge that single-RSA and three-RSA ad groups don't" ([3P] [Optmyzr, Apr 7, 2023](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/optmyzr-study-responsive-search-ad-performance/)). Update the wording in playbook and adCopy `second_ad`.
- "Short headlines: under 20 characters had lowest CPA (≈20,000 accounts, via a third party)" — **partly confirmed**. Optmyzr's 2024 study (22K+ accounts) says headlines "benefit from concision" ([3P] [Optmyzr 2024](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/google-ad-strength-study/)). The exact "<20 characters lowest CPA" bucket is in an image table I could not read. Microsoft's new ad strength wants an *average* headline length of 17–24 characters, which is compatible.
- Style rules: no unnecessary symbols, no repeated punctuation, correct capitalisation (playbook; adCopy `repeated_punctuation`, `caps`) — **confirmed** ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)). The `caps` check should allow one all-caps word and legitimate acronyms ([OFF] [Keyword capitalization](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_aboutparameterskeyword)).
- No urgency (playbook, adCopy `urgency`) — **confirmed, and stricter than the app**. Policy bans urgency phrases ("supplies are running out", "offer won't last") with no "real end date" carve-out ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)). Real deadlines belong in promotion extensions or countdown customizers. Pricing does require stating time or stock limitations that affect a price shown, but in a non-EU market block ([OFF] [Pricing](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60381/-1)).
- Superlatives need proof (adCopy `superlative`) — **confirmed**: claims and comparisons must be substantiated in the ad or on the landing page, with source and date for comparisons ([OFF] [Misleading content](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60357/-1); [OFF] [IP](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60211/-1)). The app's evidence or landing-page-quote approach matches.
- Every claim must be on the landing page (prompt, adCopy `claims_*`) — **confirmed** (Misleading content; Pricing "Advertised offers must match the landing page"; price and promotion extension policies).
- Editorial statuses Active / ActiveLimited / Inactive / Disapproved (prompt) — **confirmed**, with a correction below.
- Test with patience, judging on conversion rate and CPA rather than CTR alone — **compatible**. Microsoft's minimum is ≥1,000 impressions and ≥1 week per ad ([OFF] [About RSAs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads)).

**Contradicted or imprecise**
- Playbook: "Annoncestyrke og elementvurderinger (Lav/God/Bedst)" ("Ad strength and asset ratings (Low/Good/Best)") conflates two things. Ad strength = Poor/Average/Good/Excellent (new model: 0–100 %). Asset labels = Low/Good/Best/Unrated/Learning ([OFF] [About RSAs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivesearchads); [OFF] [AssetLink](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/assetlink?view=bingads-13)).
- adCopy `exclamation` warns on *any* "!" in a headline and says it "kan give en afvisning" ("can cause a rejection"). Policy: "One exclamation point or question mark is allowed per sentence in ad text"; only consecutive symbols are banned ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)). Downgrade it to a tip (style preference). Keep `repeated_punctuation` as a warning, or even an error.
- Prompt: "Inactive = under review". **Imprecise for new ads:** API reads of a *new* ad in offline review can return `Disapproved` while the UI shows "Pending editorial review". `Inactive` is returned for *updated* ads under review. An updated ad is effectively paused until re-approved ([OFF] [Editorial Review and Appeals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/editorial-review-appeals?view=bingads-13)). The app should not report a freshly created ad as rejected on API status alone; check the reasons with GetEditorialReasonsByIds or the delivery status.
- adCopy `phone` says phone numbers in ad text are "begrænset i flere markeder" ("restricted in several markets"), and the playbook says phone numbers only in a call extension. **Not verified** in the current (Aug 31, 2026) General Ad Requirements. Keep it as a best practice (call extension; on smartphones only in Denmark), not as a cited policy.
- Prompt build-plan step 8 assumes account-level sitelinks are "inherited" and "also show". Per current docs, higher-level extensions of the same type are eligible to serve *alongside* lower-level ones when predicted to help ([OFF] [Ad Extensions guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/ad-extensions?view=bingads-13)). The same help page still carries the old "ad group overrides" note. The app's approach (report the account-level ones) is right; the reason should be "they can serve together".
- adCopy `vat` tip says to state ekskl./inkl. moms (excl./incl. VAT) when a price appears. Microsoft's EU pricing block says prices should include all taxes, VAT included ([OFF] [Pricing](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60381/-1)). For B2C, "inkl. moms" should be the default; "ekskl. moms" only for clearly B2B offers. That B2B carve-out is Danish law and not sourced here.

**Missing (should be added)**
1. **Autogenerated RSA assets:** on by default for new RSAs since Jan 15, 2026, and being extended to existing RSAs without 15 headlines and 4 descriptions (Apr 2026). The setting is per campaign and the source is the landing page. The app should read the campaign setting, warn that Microsoft may add unreviewed text, and propose opting out, or use all 15+4 fields where claims must be controlled ([OFF] [Blog Jan 15, 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/january-2026/performance-max-updates-and-other-product-news-for-january-2026); [OFF] [Blog Apr 16, 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/april-2026/performance-max-updates-and-other-product-news-for-april-2026)).
2. **"Only one price or % allowed per ad"** — a new check: more than one distinct price or percentage in a single text (and, conservatively, across the ad) → warning ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)).
3. **ASCII-only final URLs and display paths:** no æ/ø/å or diacritics in the final URL, Path1 or Path2 → error ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)). Also: no "/" in paths, and domain+paths ≤ 67 characters ([OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)).
4. **One base domain per ad group** for all ads and keyword URLs ([OFF] [Landing Page Quality](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60215/-1)). The final URL must not return 4xx, block the crawler, or redirect to another domain ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)).
5. **Sentence case:** descriptions must be sentence case. For Danish, sentence case headlines (no English Title Case) are the safe inference ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)). adCopy could flag Title Case in Danish texts as a tip.
6. **Minimum 3 words** across title and text, and no emojis (they also count as double-width) ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1); [OFF] [ResponsiveSearchAd](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/responsivesearchad?view=bingads-13)).
7. **Dynamic-text parsing bug risk in adCopy `plain()`:** its regex `/\{[^}:]*:([^}]*)\}/` takes the text after the first ":" as the "default". For `{COUNTDOWN(2026-11-27 23:59:59,5)}` the time's colons give a wrong length. Countdown and GLOBAL_COUNTDOWN have no default text, so a separate rule is needed. `{LOCATION(City):default}`, `{KeyWord:default}` and `{param1:default}` work ([OFF] [Countdown](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_countdown); [OFF] [Location insertion](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_responsivelocation)). Also: "If you bid on misspelled keywords, do not use them in ad groups that contain {keyword}" ([OFF] [General Ad Requirements](https://help.ads.microsoft.com/#apex/ads/en/60209/-1)). Optmyzr found DKI lowers conversions per ad ([3P] [Optmyzr 2023](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/optmyzr-study-responsive-search-ad-performance/)).
8. **Extension limits and rules for `add_sitelinks` and the other extension work:**
   - Sitelink text ≤ 25 characters with descriptions (35 without); descriptions 35 + 35, both required; ≤ 20 per entity; 2–8 shown; unique link text.
   - Callouts ≤ 25 characters, at least 2 needed, 2–4 shown, not repeating the ad.
   - Snippet values ≤ 25 characters, 3–10, predefined language header, no promotional text.
   - Price 3–8 rows, header and description 25 characters each, prices on the landing page.
   - Promotion: item ≤ 20, code ≤ 15, DKK and Danish supported, occasion windows (Black Friday Oct 15–Dec 15), no audience-restricted offers, details on the landing page.
   - Call: campaign level only, one per campaign, smartphones only in DK, number must match the website.
   - Location: account or campaign only.
   - Logo: 1:1 ≥ 128 px, one per level.
   - Image: ≥ 703×368, no scheduling.
   - Review: third party ≤ 1 year old, no individual reviews.
   - API UpdateAdExtensions deletes omitted optional fields, so always send the full object.
   - Sources: section 2 above.
9. **Automated extensions:** on by default; account-level opt-out only. They include "longer ad headlines", dynamic descriptions and callouts, and merchant ratings. An automatic account logo is added from Mar 17, 2025. Autogenerated image extensions apply to ad groups without images ([OFF] [Automated extensions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_automatedextensions); [OFF] [Opt-out](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_automatedextensionsoptout); [OFF] [Logo](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_addlogoextension)).
10. **Review timing and appeals:**
    - Most reviews take ≤ 48 hours (contact support after 2 business days); RSA recommendations take < 3 hours to 1 business day; asset edits serve after about 5 minutes.
    - Appeals: UI "Request an exception" per ad, asset or extension. The API allows 2,000 per day and 10,000 pending, and extensions cannot be appealed via the API.
    - Aug 2026: bulk edit and appeal of assets in the Assets tab.
    - Sources: section 4.
11. **ETA retirement facts** for answering users: ETAs can't be created or edited since Feb 1, 2023, but existing ones still serve and can be paused. Standard text ads stopped serving in Feb 2025 ([OFF] [ETA FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_exta_faq)).
12. **Multimedia ads** as an optional add-on: sidebar positions, 3 active and 5 total per ad group, 1.91:1 and 1:1 images required ([OFF] [MMA](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_conc_mma_search)).
13. **Max CPC** (adjacent to ads): from Oct 1, 2026, new non-portfolio campaigns using Maximize Conversions (tCPA), Maximize Conversion Value (tROAS) or Maximize Clicks cannot set Max CPC ([OFF] [Blog Aug 31, 2026](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/august-2026/ai-max-for-search-and-other-product-news-for-august-2026)). Relevant to the playbook's bid sections, which are out of scope here.

### Inferences
- Of the missing items, the three with the highest risk for the Danish account are: (1) autogenerated assets adding unchecked price or "gratis" (free) claims; (2) æ/ø/å in final URLs and paths causing rejections; (3) several prices or percentages in one RSA.
